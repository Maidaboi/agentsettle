How AgentEscrow.sol works

  1. createJob(payee, deadline) — buyer posts the job → Created
  2. fundJob(jobId) payable — payment locks in escrow → Funded
  3. submitDelivery(jobId, hash) — worker stores the deliverable hash → Delivered
  4. approveAndRelease(jobId) — buyer approves → Released, reputation +1 for both agents
  5. Stuck jobs: refundAfterDeadline(jobId) refunds the buyer if nothing was delivered; claimAfterDeadline(jobId) lets a worker who delivered get paid if the buyer goes silent

State changes always happen before transfers (checks-effects-interactions), so the contract is reentrancy-safe by construction. Reputation lives in on-chain mappings: completedJobsAsPayee, completedJobsAsPayer, reputation.

HSP settlement layer

The escrow holds the job terms and reputation. Actual agent-to-agent payment settles over HSP — HashKey's merchant/agent payment protocol (docs · merchant portal). Step 5 runs HSP's Agentic Payment flow through its MCP tools, shown live in the log:

  • createPayment → getPaymentStatus → completed
  • Also available: getBalance, getFee, refundPayment, cancelPayment, getPaymentList

In this demo the HSP calls are simulated and labelled as such in the log. Live HSP settlement needs a registered merchant wallet — that integration is the next step.
